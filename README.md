# 🛒 Instacart Grocery Orders & Customer Retention Analysis

### 📋 Project Objective
Analyzed order volume and customer retention using a large grocery dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior and identify peak order times. The goal is to optimize delivery operations and improve customer retention in a highly competitive market.

### 🗂️ Data Source
* **Dataset:** [Instacart Market Basket Analysis (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/c/instacart-market-basket-analysis)

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Data Warehouse / SQL Engine:** Google BigQuery
* **Data Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI

### 📊 Key Business Insights
* **Peak Operations:** The highest order volumes occur on Days 0 and 1, surpassing 580,000 orders each. The intraday peak demand is consistently sustained between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM, with orders remaining high throughout this period.
* **Product Loyalty:** Fresh and organic produce dominate reorder rates. Bananas are the ultimate "hook products" with over 700,000 repeat purchases, followed by organic strawberries and spinach. Across 3.4M orders, there are over 19M reordered items.
* **Hidden Bulk Buyers:** While many use the service for daily needs, maximum basket sizes reached up to 145 items per order. This reveals a hidden segment of bulk-purchasers.

### 💡 Strategic Recommendations
* **Logistics Scaling:** Scale up the delivery fleet and warehouse capacity during the 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM peak period to reduce delivery delays and handle high demand.
* **Retention Campaigns:** Promote high-quality fresh and organic products through campaigns, push notifications, and in-app banners to encourage repeat purchases.
* **VIP Tier Program:** Introduce a loyalty program offering incentives like free delivery or tiered discounts for baskets exceeding 50 items to retain high-volume bulk-purchasers.
